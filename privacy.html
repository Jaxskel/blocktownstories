BlockTown Stories: privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

The BlockTown Stories uploader is a private tool used only by the BlockTown Stories team to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and privacy status on the BlockTown Stories channel, and to read that channel's basic details to confirm it is posting to the right channel.

What it stores

It stores one access token on our own computers so it can upload without signing in each time. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or other people, and it does not access any other Google account data.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Through our YouTube channel, @blocktownstoriesanimation.

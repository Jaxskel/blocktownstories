BlockTown Stories: terms of service

Last updated 7 November 2026.

The BlockTown Stories uploader is a private tool used only by the BlockTown Stories team. It publishes our own original animated videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and no one outside the team can sign in to it.

What it does

With the account owner's permission, the tool uploads finished BlockTown Stories videos with their titles and captions, and reads each account's basic profile details to confirm it is posting to the right account. It does not post on behalf of any other person or account.

Content

Every video is original work made by BlockTown Stories. We are responsible for what we publish and follow the rules of each platform, including the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Community Guidelines, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Instagram Terms of Use.

Data

How the tool handles data is described in our privacy policy. In short: it keeps only the access tokens it needs to post to our own accounts, and it collects nothing about viewers.

Changes and access

We may update these terms as the tool changes; the date above shows the latest version. An account owner can remove the tool's access at any time from that platform's settings.
